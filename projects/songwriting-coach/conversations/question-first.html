Happy to look at your hook. I'm taking it to be "Leave the kettle on", since it opens the chorus and closes it.

🟢 Memorable and specific: short, everyday and easy to hold after one listen, and a kettle is a small, real image that says "someone is waiting for me" without saying it.

🟡 Earning its repetition: the hook is three of the chorus's four lines. A listener will hear it a dozen times, so what changes each time it comes back? If the verses make "leave the kettle on" mean something new on every return, it earns it. If it means the same thing every time, it may wear thin.

🔵 Singable (provisional, no melody yet): "LEAVE the KET-tle ON" has a clear stress pattern and open vowels, which should sing easily. I can't judge it properly until there's a tune.

What do you want the kettle to mean by the final chorus that it doesn't mean the first time?

Good question. In verse one he's coming home from work. By the end he's coming home from a long time away, and nobody's left to put the kettle on.
