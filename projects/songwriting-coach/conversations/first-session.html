Since you have a full draft, the Core Assessment is a good first listen. It has 6 checks:

  1. Emotional arc review: does the lyric build, resolve, or land the way you intend?
  2. Sliver of hope check: is there any moment of lift or forward motion?
  3. Authenticity review: does it sound like a real person in a real moment?
  4. Cliche scan: flag overused words, images, or phrases
  5. Beat review: vowel quality, stress placement, syllable count against the melody
  6. Singability review: does the phrasing feel natural to perform?
