Stakes check

  • 🟢The stakes are clear from Verse 1. She says she's fine, but the porch light "burning low" shows she's still waiting.
  • 🟡Chorus: "I can't do this alone" says what she needs, not what she risks. What does she lose if she keeps walking back to him?
  • 🟡The ex is only a coffee cup. Nothing says what happened, so a listener may not know why it matters to her now.

What would you like next?

  • Emotional arc review (a Core Assessment) - does it build, resolve, or land as intended?
  • Full Stakes check report - a longer report on this check
  • Narrative arc check - do the song sections create a story with distinct phases?
